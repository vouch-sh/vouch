Get Started with Vouch

Install the CLI and enroll your YubiKey

1 Install the CLI
$brew install vouch-sh/tap/vouch
$curl -fsSL https://vouch.sh/install.sh | sh
{% if has_downloads %}

2 Enroll your YubiKey
$vouch enroll --server <your-company-url>

Your admin will give you the URL. This opens a browser to verify your identity and register your YubiKey.

3 Daily login
# Start your day with a single touch
$ vouch login
Enter PIN: ****
Touch your YubiKey...
✓ Authenticated for 8 hours

After login, your identity is available to SSH, AWS, Git, and other tools automatically.
