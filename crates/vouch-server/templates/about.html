About Vouch

What is Vouch?

Vouch is a hardware-backed authentication system that issues short-lived credentials after verifying your identity with a security key (like a YubiKey). It works with your existing identity provider (Google, Okta, etc.) - no new passwords to remember.

The core principle: no credential issuance without human presence proof. Every time you need access to systems, you physically tap your security key to prove you're there.

How it Works

  1. Sign in with your work email Through your organization's identity provider (Google, Okta, etc.)
  2. Register your security key One-time setup that links your key to your account
  3. Tap your key to get credentials SSH certificates, AWS credentials, and more - all short-lived

True Multi-Factor Authentication

Your security key provides two factors in one device:

  • Something you have: The physical hardware key
  • Something you know: Your PIN

This is stronger than password + SMS codes, which can be phished or intercepted. Hardware keys are cryptographically bound to the legitimate site and cannot be fooled by look-alike phishing pages.

Why Vouch?

  • Phishing-resistant: Hardware keys can't be phished like passwords
  • Short-lived credentials: 8-hour certificates minimize breach impact
  • No passwords to manage: Use your existing identity provider
  • Auditable: Every authentication is logged for security review
  • Simple workflow: Just tap your key when you need access
