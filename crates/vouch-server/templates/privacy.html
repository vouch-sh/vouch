Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Introduction

{{ org_name }} ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Vouch authentication service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and identity information provided through your organization's identity provider.
  • Authentication Data: FIDO2/WebAuthn credential identifiers (public keys) associated with your security keys. We never store private keys.
  • Usage Logs: Authentication events, including timestamps, IP addresses, and credential usage for security auditing.
  • Device Information: Security key metadata (e.g., authenticator type) for compatibility and security purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate your identity and issue short-lived credentials
  • Maintain security and detect unauthorized access attempts
  • Comply with legal obligations and organizational security policies
  • Improve and maintain our service

Data Retention

Authentication logs are retained according to your organization's security policies. Credential data is retained until you or your administrator removes it. Session tokens are short-lived (typically 8 hours) and automatically expire.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All communications are encrypted using TLS
  • FIDO2/WebAuthn ensures phishing-resistant authentication
  • Private keys never leave your security device
  • Short-lived credentials minimize exposure from potential breaches

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Access to your personal data
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your data (subject to retention requirements)
  • Data portability

Contact your organization's administrator to exercise these rights.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact your organization's IT administrator or security team.
